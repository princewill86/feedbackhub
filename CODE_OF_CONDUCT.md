# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss the substance of proposals and give feedback that helps others improve their work. Make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove abusive material and restrict participation.

Report behavior to a maintainer through a private channel where available. If you need a contact, open a GitHub issue asking how to reach a maintainer privately and keep incident details out of the issue.
